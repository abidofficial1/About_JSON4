ABOUT THE EVENT

Learn, Connect and Build

The SEU Tech Event is designed to bring students together to explore technology, exchange ideas and learn practical skills. The event will feature talks, demonstrations and student-oriented activities.

The main goals are to encourage collaboration, strengthen technical knowledge and create opportunities for students to connect with members of the SEU Computer Club and fellow technology enthusiasts.

Organized by SEU Computer Club

Participation Open to SEU students
